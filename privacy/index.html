Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

This policy applies to all Android apps published by BlockDMask ("we", "us"). We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Information collected by advertising partners

Our apps show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve and measure ads, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. You can learn how Google uses data in the Google partner sites policy.

Consent and your choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time from within the app's settings, where available. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Data we store

Our apps store only the data needed to work, such as your settings and in-app progress, on your device. This data is not sent to our servers.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, with the date above.
